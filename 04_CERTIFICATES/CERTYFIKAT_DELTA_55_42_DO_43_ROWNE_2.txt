C_{42}^{5,5}
=
{G : |V(G)| = 42, score_{5,5}(G)=0}

Phi_G(x)
=
Σ_{A ∈ R_4(G)} Π_{i ∈ A} x_i
+
Σ_{B ∈ B_4(G^c)} Π_{i ∈ B} (1-x_i)

Wynik certyfikatu:

Delta_{5,5}(42 -> 43)
=
min_{G ∈ C_{42}^{5,5}}
min_{x ∈ {0,1}^{42}}

2
